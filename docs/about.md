# About

Thoughtful Velocity was developed by Will Kelly, a technical content strategist focused on AI enablement, based in Northern Virginia.

Will has spent more than 15 years writing about and working inside enterprise technology, with more than 300 published bylines covering DevOps, cloud, FinOps, cybersecurity, and enterprise collaboration. His work focuses on content operations, documentation debt, AI readiness, and building knowledge systems that hold up when AI starts reading them.

Thoughtful Velocity comes out of a pattern he kept seeing across organizations: teams investing in more content while the actual constraint was how knowledge moved.
