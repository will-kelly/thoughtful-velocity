# FAQ

## Is this a maturity model?

It borrows the scoring shape of one. Each factor uses a 0 to 4 scale from nonexistent to optimized. The difference is the multiplication. A maturity model averages your strengths and weaknesses. Thoughtful Velocity tells you which weakness caps everything else.

## Can you really put a number on trust?

Not precisely. You can score it against observable behavior: owner coverage, review dates, duplicate counts, and whether people act on documentation or verify it first. The score is a way to find the constraint and argue about it with evidence. It isn't a lab measurement.

## Why not add more factors, like accuracy or accessibility?

Accuracy lives inside Trust, since people can't trust what isn't accurate. Accessibility and findability live inside Speed. Four factors are enough to locate the constraint. More factors make the model harder to use without changing the answer.

## Does this apply outside documentation?

Yes. It applies anywhere knowledge has to move between people or systems: sales enablement, support, onboarding, incident response, internal communications, and AI retrieval. The factors stay the same. The evidence changes.

## We already have a documentation team. Isn't this their job?

Documentation teams usually control Clarity and part of Speed. Trust and Reusability depend on ownership, incentives, and architecture decisions that sit with engineering, product, and leadership. That's why those two factors are so often the constraint.

## Where should we start?

Run the [self-assessment](./diagnose/self-assessment.mdx) with people from three or four teams. Find the lowest factor. Fix that before you spend money anywhere else.
